## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a hospitality-first, concierge-driven digital management interface. Combining the clarity of modern SaaS productivity with the tactility and warmth of artisanal boutique service, the aesthetic evokes ease, trust, and refined modernity.

- **Brand Personality**: Attentive, polished, breezy, and reassuring. It balances high-velocity operational speed with human curation and hospitality elegance.
- **Target Audience**: Boutique hospitality operators, culinary artisans, lifestyle retail curators, and service entrepreneurs seeking modern digital discovery without tech friction.
- **Visual Stance**: Modern Soft Minimalist meets Concierge Luxury. It leverages pristine spatial rhythm, creamy surface undertones, delicate micro-borders, and tactile interaction surfaces designed for effortless thumb navigation and scan-to-action interactions.

## Colors

The palette establishes an airy canvas grounded by high-contrast ink tones and energizing botanical and fruit-tinted accents:

- **Primary (`#1E1B4B` - Deep Midnight Indigo)**: Drives essential structural elements, primary text hierarchy, brand anchors, and prominent interactive focus.
- **Secondary (`#0D9488` - Crisp Botanical Mint)**: Highlights active states, verified status indicators, publication toggles, and catalog availability.
- **Tertiary (`#FB7185` - Radiant Soft Coral)**: Drives primary promotional callouts, featured dish markers, and critical touchpoints.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A` - Rich Ink Slate)**: Retains razor-sharp legibility across dense menu arrays and product metadata.
- **Atmospheric Pastels & Surfaces**:
  - *Base Surface*: `#FAFAF9` (warm stone cream) transitioning to `#FFFFFF` for elevated product cards.
  - *Pastel Lavender Accent*: `#F5F3FF` background with `#7C3AED` text for concierge service notes and onboarding ribbons.
  - *Pastel Buttercup*: `#FEFCE8` with `#CA8A04` for dietary notes, price tiers, and curation badges.
  - *WhatsApp Conversion Green*: `#25D366` strictly reserved for real-time concierge and direct order routing triggers.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy uses `Plus Jakarta Sans` across all roles to ensure geometric balance, open letterforms, and warm humanist terminals.

- **Display & Headlines**: Tightly tracked (`-0.03em` to `-0.015em`) with bold weights to deliver confident editorial presence on digital menus and partner landing pages.
- **Body Text**: Relaxed tracking with generous proportional line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) to provide effortless scanning of menu ingredients, allergen data, and pricing tags in low-light environments.
- **Labels & Micro-Badges**: Structured with uppercase or medium capitalization and positive tracking (`0.02em` to `0.06em`) to preserve legibility at micro dimensions (11px).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an adaptive 8-point base grid system that ensures ergonomic mobile browsing for dining guests while providing dense multi-column tools for management dashboards:

- **Phone Layout (< 640px)**: Single-column canvas with a fluid grid, `1rem` outer canvas margins, and dynamic bottom navigation safe areas. Critical bottom-pinned CTAs use sticky floating positioning to allow one-handed ordering.
- **Tablet Layout (640px - 1024px)**: 6-column fluid structure with `1.5rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters, supporting side-by-side menu categories and interactive visual previews.
- **Desktop Dashboard (> 1024px)**: 12-column structured grid capped at 1280px max width with `2.5rem` margins and `1.75rem` gutters, segregating live mobile preview canvases from inventory management lists.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy shadows, opting instead for a luminous, clean layering model supported by soft pastel fills, delicate multi-stop ambient light, and structural hairline borders.

- **Surface Level 0 (Canvas)**: Background base `#FAFAF9` with no elevation.
- **Surface Level 1 (Cards & Modules)**: Flat pure `#FFFFFF` bounded by a 1px solid border in `#F1F5F9` and lifted by an ambient whisper shadow: `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Interactive Floating Triggers & Modals)**: `#FFFFFF` or solid deep indigo `#1E1B4B` supported by a soft multi-stop spread: `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.12), 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Concierge Banners & Glass Overlays)**: Translucent milky surfaces (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`) coupled with a `12px` backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) and a `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)` inner inset highlight.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a polished, friendly curvature that feels natural to touch and modern to view:

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px)**: Reserved for micro-badges, tabular tags, and secondary action inputs.
- **Large Radius (`1rem` / 16px)**: Applied to product cards, QR presentation panels, sheet drawers, and catalog items.
- **Extra Large Radius (`1.5rem` / 24px)**: Applied to high-impact marketing tiles, concierge hero banners, and primary mobile modals.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`)**: Mandated for bottom action sheets, pill badges, and direct messaging triggers (including the WhatsApp trigger).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Deep Midnight Indigo background (`#1E1B4B`), pure white label text (`#FFFFFF`), bold font, pill-shaped (`9999px` radius), padded with `0.875rem 1.75rem`. Elevates slightly with scale transition on active press.
- **WhatsApp Direct Trigger**: `#25D366` surface with high-contrast `#FFFFFF` text and accompanying brand icon. Always renders with a pill radius and subtle pulse border highlight when customer concierge routing is active.
- **Secondary Ghost**: `#FFFFFF` background with a subtle `#E2E8F0` border, `#1E1B4B` text, and soft mint background transition on hover (`#F0FDFA`).

### Micro-Badges & Dietary Tags
- Built with a compact height (`22px` to `26px`), uppercase tracking, bold weight (`11px`), and pill radius.
- Distinct thematic pairings:
  - *Vegan / Fresh*: `#CCFBF1` (Mint Tint) with `#0F766E` text.
  - *Chef's Special / Highlight*: `#FFE4E6` (Soft Peach) with `#E11D48` text.
  - *Concierge Managed*: `#EDE9FE` (Lavender Tint) with `#6D28D9` text.
  - *Limited Batch*: `#FEF08A` (Soft Yellow) with `#854D0E` text.

### Catalog & Menu Cards
- Elevated Level 1 surface (`#FFFFFF`), `1rem` corner rounding, and `1px` subtle outline (`#F1F5F9`).
- Features a dual-zone horizontal orientation on mobile (thumbnail at `88x88px` with `0.75rem` radius on the trailing edge, textual description and price lockups on the leading edge).
- Integrated quick-action toggles: single-tap heart favorites, instant WhatsApp inquiry links, and stock availability switches.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background is `#FFFFFF` with a crisp `1.5px` border in `#E2E8F0`. Focus state shifts smoothly to `#1E1B4B` with a `4px` focus-ring halo tinted in `#EEF2FF`.
- Labels sit outside the field using `label-md` typography to maximize clarity during high-speed menu editing.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- Segmented category switches utilize a pill-shaped track in `#F1F5F9` with a sliding `#FFFFFF` thumb that gains a delicate `0px 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)` shadow.
- Checkboxes use rounded square profiles (`6px` radius) transitioning from clean border outlines to solid `#0D9488` when toggled.

### QR Code Generator & Showcase Tile
- Centered showcase module framed by high-contrast pastel padding, featuring high-resolution SVG QR renders, custom business logo centers, and one-tap print/export download triggers.
